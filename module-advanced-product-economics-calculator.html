Nightly Profit Margin

Does adding Seated guests to a night of service help or hurt, in dollars and in margin %?

Go do this in the live tool first

  1. On the "Nightly Profit Margin" tab, with the default numbers untouched, read the callout: does blended margin go up or down when Seated guests are added? By how much?
  2. Slowly raise the "Seated reward rate" input. Find the point where the callout flips from "margin improves" to "margin dips." What rate did that happen at, and why?
  3. Set "Guests without Seated" to 0, leaving Seated guests at 10. What happens to the "Without Seated" column's profit and margin, and why does that make sense given fixed costs don't disappear?

Part 2 of 4

The Cost of an Empty Table

What is an empty seat actually costing the restaurant, and what would filling some of them be worth?

Go do this in the live tool first

  1. On "The Cost of an Empty Table" tab, enter a realistic week (or use the defaults). What's the weekly gross profit left on the table? What's that annualized?
  2. Look at the "Occupancy by day" bars. Which day is weakest, and by how much (in missed gross profit) compared to the strongest day?
  3. Drag the "Share of empty seats captured" slider. At roughly what % capture does the added annual gross profit cross $500,000?

Part 3 of 4

New Guest Economics

Is Seated bringing incremental business, or just serving people who'd have come anyway?

Go do this in the live tool first

  1. With defaults, read the breakeven repeat-guest rate from the stat card. Does "the simple way to think about it" match "Net new profit from Seated" above it? (It should, always.)
  2. Lower "New guest rate" until "Net new profit from Seated" turns negative. What rate caused it, and how does that compare to the breakeven rate at the default reward rate?
  3. In your own words, explain to a partner why a "regular" guest still costs money even though they generate zero incremental sales.

Profit Margin by Occupancy

How does margin trend as occupancy climbs, where Seated is responsible for filling seats above baseline?

Go do this in the live tool first

  1. With defaults, read the "Breakeven occupancy" stat card. Find that same crossing point in the chart/table below -- which occupancy column is the first with a positive (gold) margin?
  2. Drag "Baseline occupancy" down to 50%. Does breakeven occupancy go up or down? Why, in terms of how much of the chart is now "Seated fills the rest" territory?
  3. Look at the chart's leftmost bars (very low occupancy, deep red, hatched). Why doesn't a -410% margin mean the model is broken?
