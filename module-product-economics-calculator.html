How to use this module

  1. Click "Make your copy" above -- Google will prompt you to save your own version to your Drive.
  2. In your copy, you'll see 6 yellow input cells: Average Spend, Guests w/o Seated, Seated Guests, F&B %, Seated Reward Rate, and Fixed Costs.
  3. For each scenario below, change those 6 inputs to match the numbers given, and read the answer off the calculated columns to the right.
  4. Answer the question, then move to the next scenario. Each scenario changes ALL 6 inputs, so double check every field before reading your answer.
