Month-end outcomes

Logged by each rep in the first week of the month. Restaurants and points against goal, the share of restaurants not paid on, and take rate against the 18% standard.

Knowledge

Average quiz score on the modules due so far for the rep's stage. An overdue module counts as 0 until it's done.

Effort

Commitments met each week, out of 5: check-in on time, working-on goal done (Partly counts half), and each of the 3 number goals.

Outcome

Share of the standards for the rep's stage they hit each week. Month-end restaurants, points and take rate are tracked in the table above.

Reps and managers

Only admins see this tab. Changes here update every view straight away. Reps set their own manager on their first check-in; fix it here if it's wrong. Emails fill in from first initial + last name.
