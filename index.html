Seated Certification Program

Eleven modules, organized by the month they need to be completed. Each one scores and saves on submission.

Month 0

Before ramp begins

  • Restaurants 101 ~20–25 min

    The industry, restaurant lingo, operator types, and the reservation platform landscape. 23-question quiz plus 3 exercises.

  • Cold Call Training ~15–20 min

    Mindset, discovery, the ARP framework, and the gatekeeper. 17-question quiz plus 3 exercises.

  • Objection Handling ~10 min

    11 real objections straight from the Cold Call Hub, and the correct response to each.

Month 1

First month on the job

  • API Partners ~15 min

    Every card issuer, bank, and rewards platform in Seated's dining network. 23-question quiz plus a partner match exercise.

  • Competitive Landscape ~15 min

    The tech stack, key competitors, and how Seated positions against them. 17-question quiz plus 2 exercises.

  • Ramping Quota, Commission & Expectations ~15–20 min

    Daily KPIs, ramp milestones, the comp plan, and performance expectations. 11-question quiz plus 5 exercises.

  • Setting Meetings That Hold ~10 min

    Turning conversations into confirmed bookings that actually show up. 10-question quiz plus the post-call cadence exercise.

  • Simple Product Economics Calculator ~15–20 min

    Make a copy of the live calculator, run 3 real scenarios, and learn the incremental math behind every pitch.

Month 2

Deepening the fundamentals

  • Advanced Product Economics Calculator ~25–30 min

    The full economics toolkit, hands-on: nightly margin, empty-table cost, new guest economics, and margin by occupancy. 4 live-tool exercises plus 10 questions.

  • Advanced Objection Handling ~20–25 min

    Pricing, cannibalization, competitors, and order form terms. 26-question quiz plus 3 exercises.

  • Pre-Pitch Research ~10 min

    What to know before you ever get on the call.

  • Pipeline Management ~15–20 min

    Stages, hygiene, the missed meeting protocol, and the Monday weekly reset. 10-question quiz plus 4 exercises.
