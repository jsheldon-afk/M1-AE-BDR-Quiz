Seated Coaching Hub

You own your numbers and your plan to hit them. Your manager owns the coaching plan that gets you there. The Coaching Hub gives you both the same picture of where you stand, what's holding you back, and what to do next.

Each part feeds the next, and Outcome naturally comes from Knowledge and Effort working together. When you know why and how a behavior makes you successful, you do it consistently. When you do the behaviors consistently, the numbers follow. And when a number is short, you and your manager already have a plan for how to improve.

Who owns what

You own

  • Your numbers. You log them yourself every week, so you always know them.
  • Your plan. You set your goals and decide how you'll hit them and how you'll track it.
  • Your pipeline. Stages up to date in Salesforce before every check-in.
  • Speaking up early. If you're off track midweek, you say so before Friday.

Your manager owns

  • Your coaching plan. Built around your biggest gap, not a general pep talk.
  • Agreeing your goals. Pressure-testing your plan in your Monday 1:1.
  • Course-correcting with you. Answering when you reply to the Wednesday email.
  • Making sure you can win. The knowledge, tools and time to do the job.
